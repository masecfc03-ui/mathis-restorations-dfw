Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Do you work with insurance companies?
Yes, we work with all major carriers. We'll come out, document the damage, and walk you through the claims process. We handle the back-and-forth so you can focus on your family.
How soon can you get out for an inspection?
We typically schedule free inspections within 24–48 hours. After a major storm event, call us immediately — we prioritize active damage calls.
Are you licensed and insured in Texas?
Yes. We carry full general liability and workers' comp insurance and are licensed for roofing and renovation work throughout Texas. We provide certificates of insurance before any job starts.
What roofing materials do you recommend?
For most DFW homes, we recommend Class 4 impact-resistant architectural shingles — they hold up better against hail and often qualify for insurance discounts. We carry GAF, CertainTeed, and Owens Corning lines.
